# 1.3 Culture

## Summary

Our core values are Peace, Wisdom, Deliberation, Respect, and Agency. They guide how we take initiative and work together.

## 1.3.1 Core values {#core-values}

**Peace.** We are peaceful activists. We do not harm people or property, or threaten to do so.

**Wisdom.** The fight for democracy is a marathon, not a sprint. We take care of ourselves and others.

**Deliberation.** We consider a range of tactics, disagree openly, and incorporate feedback.

**Respect.** We are kind, listen, and use nonviolent communication.

**Agency.** When we feel strongly, we take action.

## 1.3.2 Operating principles {#operating-principles}

**Abundant leadership.** Everyone can develop as a leader. Take initiative, think about strategy, and help resolve conflict.

**Distributed responsibilities.** Share responsibilities and make them clear so work doesn't depend on one person.

**Tactical freedom.** Within our values and agreed campaigns, teams can try different ways to advance the mission. Check decision rights before making commitments for the organization.

**Collective learning.** Test ideas, reflect on what happened, and share what others can learn. Written documentation helps useful knowledge travel.

## 1.3.4 Culture in daily work {#culture-in-daily-work}

Apply the values to concrete choices. Before an action, consider risks to participants and the public. When a plan is contested, listen to the disagreement and explain the tradeoffs. When work depends on information you hold, share the context so others can act. If you can no longer fulfill a responsibility, arrange a handover rather than letting it become a bottleneck.

These examples explain the principles. Check [decision rights](structure.md#roles-circles-decision-rights) before making organizational commitments. For practical conversations, see [3.1.1 Feedback culture](../leadership/culture-in-leadership.md#feedback-culture).

## 1.3.5 What is distinctive about our culture? {#distinctive-culture}

Our culture pairs room for initiative with shared values, clear responsibilities, and collective learning.
