# E.2 Compensation

## Summary

This page names the factors that may shape staff compensation: location, role, and performance. No pay policy, benchmark, role band, formula, or amount has been adopted, and nothing here is a promise of pay. One principle is settled: performance is assessed against the roles a person actually holds, using [E.1 Expectations](expectations.md). How scores relate to pay is undecided.

## E.2.1 Location {#location}

Location may affect compensation; whether and how is not yet decided.

## E.2.2 Role {#role}

Staff can hold Individual Contributor, Directly Responsible Individual, and Player-Coach roles, and these overlap. [E.1.4 Mixed roles and predominant role](expectations.md#mixed-roles) notes that IC, DRI, and Player-Coach are not a managerial ladder and that how to determine a predominant role is not yet decided. Whether predominant role affects pay is also open.

## E.2.3 Performance {#performance}

Performance is assessed against the rubrics in [E.1 Expectations](expectations.md). Per that page, a 3 means fully meeting expectations, and performance is kept separate from promotion. How a performance rating affects pay, if at all, is not yet decided.

## E.2.4 Worked example {#worked-example}

Once the policy exists, this section will work through an example: a person in India whose predominant role is DRI and whose performance is rated 3 out of 5.

## E.2.5 Review and administration {#review-and-administration}

Review timing, who approves changes, and how exceptions are handled are not yet decided.
