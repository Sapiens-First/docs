# D Glossary

## Summary

This page defines the words the handbook uses, in alphabetical order, each linking to where it is explained in full. "Organizer" is the umbrella term for people taking ongoing responsibility; Fellow, Steward, and Lead are legacy terms that point to the Individual Contributor, Directly Responsible Individual, and Player-Coach role types. Atlas holds current roles and role holders. Not sure what a word means? Look it up here first, and add new organizational terms as you meet them.

## D.1 Alphabetical terms {#alphabetical-terms}

### Accountability

One of a role's ongoing responsibilities, recorded as part of that role in Atlas. A role can have several accountabilities. See [Roles and circles](../dna/structure.md#roles-circles-decision-rights).

### Atlas

Our live record of current governance, roles, projects, priorities, and owners — the handbook explains concepts, while [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas) shows who is doing what right now. See [The handbook and Atlas](../introduction/how-the-handbook-works.md#where-authoritative-information-lives).

### Campaign

A concrete piece of advocacy work people can get involved in, such as a policy push or a local action series. Current campaigns and their resources are listed at [sapiensfirst.org/campaigns](https://sapiensfirst.org/campaigns). See [Peaceful actions](../dna/strategy.md#act).

### Chapter

A larger stage of local organizing than a circle, described in source material alongside circles, hubs, and alliances. The thresholds and procedures for becoming a chapter still need confirmation before they're adopted rules. See [Local circles](../dna/structure.md#local-circles-and-growth).

### Circle

A group of roles brought together around a shared purpose. A **local circle** is a small group that meets and acts together in one place. See [Roles and circles](../dna/structure.md#roles-circles-decision-rights) and [Local circles](../dna/structure.md#local-circles-and-growth).

### Decision rights

What a role or project owner can **decide** alone, where they should **consult** affected people, and which choices someone else must **approve**. See [Decisions](../dna/structure.md#roles-circles-decision-rights).

### Domain

An area of related work that Atlas groups roles and projects under, alongside its views for roles and people. See [Finding current people, projects, and contacts](../introduction/getting-involved.md#finding-current-people-and-projects).

### Directly Responsible Individual (DRI)

A person who owns an outcome, especially one spanning functions, systems, or contributors, with a clear purpose, domain, accountabilities, and decision authority. See [E.1.2 Directly Responsible Individual](staff/expectations.md#directly-responsible-individual).

### Fellow

**Legacy term** for an organizer in the foundational reading series; the closest role type is the [Individual Contributor](#individual-contributor-ic). Someone taking part in the Fellowship: a structured volunteer program in which they take responsibility for a project, with support. See [The Fellowship](../introduction/getting-involved.md#volunteer-and-fellowship-pathways).

### Fellowship

A structured volunteer program, not employment, in which each Fellow takes responsibility for a project, with support. See [The Fellowship](../introduction/getting-involved.md#volunteer-and-fellowship-pathways).

### Individual Contributor (IC)

A person who directly creates value through their craft. See [E.1.1 Individual Contributor](staff/expectations.md#individual-contributor).

### Key result

The measure that shows an objective's outcome happened, also called a success criterion. Each key result should point to a defined metric — usually one that measures the outcome. See [How Sapiens First manages work](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#okrs).

### Member

Someone who participates in the community, helps with events, and builds connections, one of the ways to get involved with Sapiens First. See [Ways to participate](../dna/structure.md#participation-responsibility-employment).

### Metric

A clearly defined measure — typed as an input, output, or outcome — with a starting point, a target, and a date, useful when it helps someone decide better. See [Metrics](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#metrics).

### Mission, pillar, program

How Atlas organizes work: the **mission** is the overall change we work toward, and a **pillar** is a major contribution to it. A **program** groups related work around a continuing purpose. See [From mission to work](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#from-mission-to-objectives).

### Objective

A change we want to achieve — it names the outcome we want. See [Outcomes and outputs](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#okrs).

### Organizer

Someone taking ongoing responsibility for organizing: turning shared concerns into collective action, with clear responsibilities and support from one another. Organizer is the umbrella term for the role types Individual Contributor, Directly Responsible Individual, and Player-Coach; the older titles Fellow, Steward, and Lead are legacy terms. See [Organizers and role types](../dna/structure.md#participation-responsibility-employment) and [Where to start as an organizer](../introduction/getting-involved.md#where-to-start-as-an-organizer).

### Output and outcome

An **output** is something we create, like a workshop. An **outcome** is the change it leads to, like participants being able to run their first meeting — the change an objective names. [Metrics](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#metrics) uses these words the same way, and pairs them with **input**, an early measure of effort.

### Input

An early-stage measure a role holder can directly influence, such as how quickly new sign-ups are contacted. See [Metrics](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#metrics).

### Player-Coach (PC)

A person who combines substantive work in their craft with developing other people. See [E.1.3 Player-Coach](staff/expectations.md#player-coach).

### Product or service

Something people can use or benefit from, such as a resource center or a training offering. It can need several projects over its life.

### Project

A bounded effort to create or change something. See [Projects](../leadership/strategic-planning.md#project-portfolio-management).

### Red lines

Things people acting in the name of Sapiens First must never do, such as threatening harm or harassing others. See [Values](../appendices/reference/values-standards.md).

### Role

A defined responsibility, with a purpose, accountabilities, and a scope. A person can hold several roles, and who fills a role can change. See [Roles and circles](../dna/structure.md#roles-circles-decision-rights).

### Role holder

The person currently filling a role, as recorded in Atlas.

### Scope

What a project includes and excludes, with its objective, success criteria, constraints, and decision rights. See the [project scope template](reference/planning-templates.md#project-scope).

### Staff

People paid to work for Sapiens First. Staff status is separate from participation level, governance role, and performance role. See [E.0.4 What counts as staff?](staff/beliefs-about-staff.md#what-counts-as-staff)

### Lead

**Legacy term**; the closest role type is the [Player-Coach](#player-coach-pc). An organizer taking on leadership responsibilities. See [Organizers and role types](../dna/structure.md#participation-responsibility-employment).

### Steward

**Legacy term**; the closest role type is the [Directly Responsible Individual](#directly-responsible-individual-dri). A further engagement level described in Atlas for Fellows after three months and graduation. A role with "Steward" in its title is a separate thing from this engagement level. See [Ways to participate](../dna/structure.md#participation-responsibility-employment).

### Supporter

Someone who stays informed and joins activities when they can, the lightest way to get involved with Sapiens First. See [Ways to participate](../dna/structure.md#participation-responsibility-employment).

### Tension

A gap between how things are and how they could be, raised with the responsible role or circle. See [Atlas and AI](../dna/structure.md#atlas-and-the-handbook).
