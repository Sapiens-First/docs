# Version log

> What changed in the handbook, release by release, newest first.

## In brief

- Versions use **CalVer**, `YYYY.0M.MICRO`: the year, the zero-padded month, and a counter that starts at 0 each month (`2026.09.0`, `2026.09.1`, `2026.10.0`).
- A release is a day of meaningful reader-facing change, not every edit. Typo fixes and tooling work are folded into the next release.
- The current version is shown in the **More** menu and in the home page footer.

::: related
- [How to use this handbook](introduction/welcome.md) — Where to start, and how the handbook relates to Atlas.
- [Find anything in Sapiens First](introduction/how-the-handbook-works.md#find-anything) — Common questions routed to the page that answers them.
:::

## Unreleased

Not yet versioned. These changes are in the working copy and will ship in the next release.

**Headline: the whole handbook as a download, and versioned releases.**

- The full handbook can be downloaded as one HTML page, a PDF or an EPUB from the **More** menu.
- A reading progress bar shows how far through a page you are, and long pages end with a **Back to top** link.
- Releases now carry a CalVer version, recorded on this page.
- The numbered handbook is now the only structure. The older audience pages (For Supporters, For Members, For Organizers and their guides) have been retired; their content lives in the numbered chapters and appendices, and old links redirect to the new pages.

## 2026.10.0

**2026-10-02: the numbered staff handbook begins, and pages are easier to read and search.**

- A numbered handbook now sits beside the existing guides, starting with the **Introduction**, which explains the purpose of the handbook and how to read it.
- **DNA** opens with the strategy chapter and the organizing cycle.
- **Staff** pages set out expectations for individual contributors, DRIs and player-coaches, plus department-specific guidance.
- **Reference materials** hold the canonical expectation rubrics and the tech team meeting agenda. Staff pages reuse the rubric text, so it is written once and stays consistent.
- Navigation is generated from one ordered contents list, so numbering and menus stay in step.
- A redesigned reading experience: navigation fits at every screen width, the home page has one set of audience cards plus a large search box, and each page shows one status badge, a summary panel, clearer headings and quieter section numbers.
- Search ranks titles and headings higher, headings have copy-link buttons, a missing page shows a helpful 404, and pages print cleanly.

## 2026.09.1

**2026-09-28: easier to find things.**

- The sidebar is regrouped by subject, and a new **Find anything** page routes common questions to the page that answers them.
- The home page routes readers by subject, with the most-sought pages linked directly.
- Page titles now make sense out of context, for example in search results.
- Gaps found in testing were closed: how to propose a project, project authority, metric terminology, chapters and the theory of change.
- Newcomers are pointed to gatherings as a first step.

## 2026.09.0

**2026-09-27: the first unified handbook.**

- The movement guide and the Fellowship guide are combined into one handbook with a shared look matching sapiensfirst.org.
- Pages and sections were renamed to a consistent scheme, and every page was rewritten for readability: opening framing, tighter prose, and skimmable structure.
- A new **Strategic hypotheses** page explains how to work backwards from outcomes.
- Pages show their status and last-updated date, and readers and AI tools can use machine-readable copies of the handbook.
- The decision guide is drawn left to right so it stays readable on phones.
