# 0.1 Welcome to Sapiens First

## Summary

Sapiens First brings people together to fight for democracy, prosperity, and security in the age of AI. This handbook supports learning throughout your involvement: understand the movement, practice organizing, take responsibility, and help others learn. Start with a short reading path or return to the section that answers your current question.

## 0.1.1 Who we are and why this handbook exists {#who-we-are}

We want AI to serve the common good. Sapiens First organizes for democratic renewal, common prosperity, and a secure future through public participation and peaceful collective action.

The handbook gives people shared language, explanations, and practical guidance. Use it to understand our approach, find a contribution, and coordinate with others. Learn, try a useful next step, reflect with others, and return to the material as you grow. For the account of our purpose and approach, read [1.1 Story](../dna/story.md) and [1.2 Strategy](../dna/strategy.md).

## 0.1.3 How to read it {#how-to-read-it}

**Start with the basics.** Every page opens with a Summary that gives what you need to act. Continue through a chapter for context, or jump to a numbered section when you need a specific answer. You don't need to read everything at once.

**Open the details when you need them.** Collapsible sections labelled **For role holders** or **Background** hold the extra depth.

**Choose a reading path.** The table above and [0.3.4](getting-involved.md#where-to-start-as-an-organizer) link to the numbered material. Return after a conversation, action, or training to review what worked and choose what to learn next. The reading paths are guides to learning, not restrictions on who may read a page.

**Check what is ready.** Planned pages name future modules; they do not yet provide a training curriculum. Drafts and pages under construction explain what is available and what still needs agreement.

**Look up any term.** The [Glossary](../appendices/glossary.md) explains the words we use.

**Find a specific answer.** Use site search or [Find anything](how-the-handbook-works.md#find-anything). For current people, projects, and priorities, consult [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas).
