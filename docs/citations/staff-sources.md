## C.3.1 Expectations (E.1) {#expectations}

Named in the October 2 performance notes ("Main references") and cited from [E.1](../appendices/staff/expectations.md). Unverified: bibliographic details, relevant passages, and the claims it supports have not been checked.

- Block / Jack Dorsey and Roelof Botha, [*From Hierarchy to Intelligence*](https://block.xyz/inside/from-hierarchy-to-intelligence): source of the IC / DRI / Player-Coach role architecture and the broader argument for replacing information-routing hierarchy with distributed context and ownership.
- Locke and Latham, goal-setting theory: specific, challenging expectations plus feedback improve attention, effort, persistence, and strategy formation relative to vague expectations.
- Andy Grove, *High Output Management*: managerial effectiveness should be understood through the output and capability of the organization a manager influences, rather than the manager's personal activity.
- Holacracy, Organizational Structure: roles belong to the work rather than permanently to individual people; one individual can fill multiple roles, each with a purpose, accountabilities, and potentially domains and authorities.
