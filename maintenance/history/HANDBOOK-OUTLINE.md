## How to edit this outline

- **[EXISTING]**: usable material exists; still needs review and rewriting.
- **[NEW]**: supplied in your new outline; the old handbook does not fully cover it.
- **[MISSING: …]**: content or a decision you need to supply. Write your answer beside the marker.
- **[PROPOSED]**: an addition or placement suggested in this first pass; keep, change, or remove it.
- **[RESEARCH]**: verify the source or claim before drafting it as fact.

Numbering is Chapter → Section → Subsection: `1` → `1.2` → `1.2.3`. Appendices use `A` → `A.2` → `A.2.3`. Chapter 0 is Introduction; Chapter 3 is Staff, with beliefs about staff at 3.0 as requested. By default, each section is a page and subsections are named headings within it. Chapter landing pages summarize and route to sections. Summary, date, references, and Further Reading are page elements, not extra numbered content units.
